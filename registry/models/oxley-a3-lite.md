# Oxley-A3-Lite

A compact instruction-tuned chat model for paraphrase and rewrite generation.
Positioned as a low-cost alternative to larger hosted chat models.

## Summary

Commercial use is supported.

## Usage

Intended for batch generation of rewritten sentence pairs and short-form
editing tasks. Quantized weights are published separately.
